# Privacy Policy — Maily

Maily is a personal desktop email application that runs entirely on your own computer.

- Maily connects to your Google account using Google's official OAuth sign-in, with credentials you provide, only to display and manage your own email in the app.
- Your emails, account tokens and settings are stored only on your own computer, in the app's local data folder. Nothing is uploaded to, or stored on, any server operated by the developer.
- Maily does not collect analytics, does not track you, and does not share your data with any third party.
- You can remove an account or delete the app's local data at any time from within the app.
